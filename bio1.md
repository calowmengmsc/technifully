*Where in the world are the best biogas and biocoal industries?*

If by **biogas** you mean anaerobic digestion and biomethane, and by **biocoal** you mean biochar, torrefied biomass, and other solid biomass fuels, then the world leaders are not necessarily the same countries.
### Best biogas industries
#### 🇩🇪 Germany
Often regarded as the world's most mature biogas industry.

- Thousands of biogas plants.

- Strong integration with farming and the electricity grid.

- Historically the largest producer in Europe.

- Germany alone contributed about 20% of global biogas production in recent years according to industry reporting. [[worldbioga...iation.org]](https://www.worldbiogasassociation.org/iea-forecasts-32-growth-in-coming-years-for-biogas-sector/)

**Strengths:** engineering expertise, agricultural digestion, gas-grid integration.
#### 🇩🇰 Denmark
Probably the best example of a modern, efficient biomethane economy.

- Around a quarter of Danish natural gas consumption was already being replaced by biomethane by 2022. [[uabio.org]](https://uabio.org/en/materials/analytics/14227/)

- Extensive use of manure and agricultural residues.

- Large-scale upgrading of biogas to pipeline-quality biomethane.

**Strengths:** manure utilisation, low methane leakage, high renewable-gas penetration.
#### 🇫🇷 France
The fastest-growing biomethane market in Europe.

- Biomethane production growth has been among the strongest in Europe. [[uabio.org]](https://uabio.org/en/materials/analytics/14227/)

- Large pipeline injection programme.

- Hundreds of projects in development. [[uabio.org]](https://uabio.org/en/materials/analytics/14227/)

**Strengths:** policy support, farm-scale biomethane, rapid expansion.
#### 🇬🇧 United Kingdom
A significant producer with strong expertise in agricultural digestion and renewable gas.

- One of the larger European biogas markets. [[iea.org]](https://www.iea.org/data-and-statistics/charts/biogas-production-by-country-and-end-use-for-selected-european-countries-2024-and-2030)[[uabio.org]](https://uabio.org/en/materials/13496/)

- Strong development of biomethane injection into gas networks.
#### 🇺🇸 United States
Perhaps the most commercially attractive market.

- Huge potential from landfills, wastewater treatment and livestock operations.

- The IEA identifies the U.S. as having the largest biogas potential among advanced economies. [[iea.org]](https://www.iea.org/reports/outlook-for-biogas-and-biomethane/key-findings)

**Strengths:** renewable natural gas (RNG), landfill gas, transport fuel markets.
#### 🇨🇳 China and 🇮🇳 India
The giants in terms of future growth potential.

- Among the world's largest sustainable biogas resource bases. [[iea.org]](https://www.iea.org/reports/outlook-for-biogas-and-biomethane/key-findings)

- Massive agricultural residues and organic waste streams.

- India, Brazil and China are highlighted as leading countries in future potential. [[iea.org]](https://www.iea.org/reports/outlook-for-biogas-and-biomethane/key-findings)[[biomassafeiten.nl]](https://www.biomassafeiten.nl/en/2026/08/04/IEA-maps-global-biogas-and-biomethane-potential/)

### Best biocoal / biochar industries
#### 🇺🇸 United States
Currently one of the most advanced biochar markets.

- Strong carbon-credit ecosystem.

- Commercial-scale producers.

- Significant research and agricultural deployment. [[forestresources.org]](https://forestresources.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/26-TR-21-Biochar-2026-Where-are-We-and-What-are-the-Opportunities.pdf)
#### 🇨🇦 Canada
A major innovator in forestry-based biochar.

- Large biomass resource.

- Strong links between forestry and carbon-removal markets.
#### 🇦🇺 Australia
One of the most active countries in biochar deployment.

- Soil improvement in dry climates.

- Strong carbon farming incentives.
#### 🇫🇮 Finland and 🇸🇪 Sweden
Leaders in torrefied biomass and forest-based bioenergy.

- Extensive forestry sectors.

- Development of bio-coal substitutes for industrial use, especially steelmaking and district heating.
#### 🇧🇷 Brazil
Enormous biomass resource.

- Sugar-cane bagasse and agricultural residues.

- Potential for both biochar and renewable solid fuels.
#### 🇨🇳 China
A rapidly expanding biochar producer.

- Large agricultural residue streams.

- Growing industrial-scale pyrolysis sector.

The latest International Biochar Initiative survey found that **Asia became the world's leading biochar-producing region**, overtaking North America. [[biochar-in...tional.org]](https://biochar-international.org/news/global-biochar-production-surges-nearly-threefold-as-industry-enters-new-era-of-scale/)[[biochartoday.com]](https://biochartoday.com/news/the-international-biochar-initiative-publishes-global-market-report-tracking-scale-and-commercial-evolution/)

### If I Had to Pick the World's Leaders Today

| Category | Leading Country/Region |
|-----------|-----------|
| Most mature biogas industry | 🇩🇪 Germany |
| Most effective biomethane deployment | 🇩🇰 Denmark |
| Fastest biomethane growth | 🇫🇷 France |
| Largest advanced-economy resource potential | 🇺🇸 United States |
| Greatest future growth potential | 🇮🇳 India, 🇨🇳 China, 🇧🇷 Brazil |
| Strongest commercial biochar sector | 🇺🇸 United States |
| Leading forest-based biocoal and bioenergy sector | 🇫🇮 Finland, 🇸🇪 Sweden |
| Fastest-growing biochar-producing region | Asia |

*Source for biochar regional growth:* International Biochar Initiative and Biochar Today market reports.

From a hydrogen and heavy-transport perspective, Denmark's biomethane model is especially noteworthy. It demonstrates how agricultural wastes and livestock manure can displace significant quantities of fossil natural gas using much of the existing gas-distribution infrastructure. For countries seeking rapid emissions reductions without waiting for a complete transition to hydrogen or battery-electric systems, biomethane may offer one of the most practical near-term decarbonisation pathways.
